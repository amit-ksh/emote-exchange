# Emote Exchange

A play-money market inside a Twitch channel where each emote is a stock whose price follows how much of chat's attention it holds.

## Language

### Players and money

**Trader**:
A Chatter who has a balance and can trade on the Exchange.
_Avoid_: investor, player, user

**Coins**:
The Exchange's play money: whole numbers, with no value outside the channel.
_Avoid_: points, credits, money

**Holding**:
How many shares of one Listing a Trader owns.
_Avoid_: position, stake

**Portfolio**:
All of a Trader's Coins and Holdings.
_Avoid_: account, wallet

**Net Worth**:
A Trader's Coins plus the current value of their Holdings.
_Avoid_: balance (balance means Coins only)

### The market

**Listing**:
An emote that is tradable on the Exchange.
_Avoid_: stock, asset, security

**Ticker**:
The name a Listing is traded by: the emote's own name.
_Avoid_: symbol

**IPO**:
The moment an emote becomes a Listing, after enough different Chatters use it.
_Avoid_: launch, listing event

**Use**:
One Chat Message containing an emote, counted toward that emote's Heat.
_Avoid_: mention, hit

**Heat**:
A Listing's recent Use, fading over time, with each Chatter's repeats counting less.
_Avoid_: hype, volume, popularity

**Attention Share**:
A Listing's share of all Listings' Heat.
_Avoid_: market share

**Price**:
What one share of a Listing costs right now, driven by its Attention Share.
_Avoid_: value

**Trade**:
A Trader buying or selling shares of a Listing at the current Price.
_Avoid_: order (there are no orders waiting to fill), transaction

**Fee**:
The small cut of every Trade that leaves the market.
_Avoid_: tax, commission

### Rewards

**Stipend**:
Coins a Trader earns for each minute they take part in chat.
_Avoid_: salary, dividend

**Raid Visa**:
The Coins bonus for first-time Chatters arriving just after a Raid.
_Avoid_: raid bonus

### Market moments

**Halt**:
A short pause in trading one Listing after its Price jumps or falls too fast.
_Avoid_: freeze (Freeze is for Traders), circuit breaker

**Moon**:
A Listing reaching a large multiple of its Session Open Price.

**Crash**:
A Listing falling to a small fraction of its Session Open Price.

**Delist**:
Removing a Listing from the Exchange and paying its holders out.
_Avoid_: remove, ban

**Freeze**:
A Mod stopping one Trader from trading.
_Avoid_: ban, suspend

**Pause**:
A Mod stopping all trading on the Exchange.

**Session Open Price**:
A Listing's Price when the current Stream Session's opening Bell rang, or its IPO Price if it listed after that Bell.

**Bell**:
The ceremony that opens or closes the market for a Stream Session.

**Session Summary**:
The closing Bell's report of the top gainer, the biggest crash and the richest Trader.

**Season**:
The period between two resets of the whole Exchange.
_Avoid_: round, era
