# Emote Exchange launch video

App-only feature film. Sarah narration from ElevenLabs. Light cream and lime motion graphics inspired by the owner's Ion reference, with actual Twitch-connected OBS recordings embedded in separate feature chapters. No build, source-code or test-results segment.

## Voiceover

Every stream has its own language. Its emotes. Its regulars. Its moments. What if chat could play with all of it?

Meet Emote Exchange. A live emote market where your community's attention moves the Prices. Coins are free play points, with no cash value.

Buy into the moment. Type buy, an emote name, and a quantity. Your shares arrive, your balance updates, and the bot confirms the Trade.

Ready to make a move? Sell some shares, or sell them all. The Coins return to your balance.

Know where you stand. Portfolio shows your Coins, Holdings, Net Worth, and current Streak. P F is the shortcut.

Make showing up count. Check in with greetings and a few words. Claim once per Stream: fifty Coins to start. Return on consecutive Streams to grow your Streak, and earn up to a hundred and forty.

Give your regulars a boost. The streamer can grant Coins from the dashboard, review the amount, and confirm. Five hundred Coins, delivered straight to the Trader, with a reply in chat.

Find your next move. Market shows the hottest tickers and how to play. Top shows the leading Traders. One command. A shared scoreboard.

Keep the conversation going. Qualifying chat earns a minute-by-minute Stipend. Follows earn a bonus. New chatters arriving after a Raid can receive a Raid Visa.

Behind every moment, the streamer stays in control. Pause trading. Freeze a Trader. Watch engagement, participation, and the economy on the Live page.

Buy. Sell. Check in. Build your Streak. Reward your community. Emote Exchange. Chat is the market.

## Production notes

- Feature commands appear as exact on-screen examples, rather than relying on spoken punctuation.
- Check-in means `!greetings <words>` once per open Stream, not a calendar-day `!daily` reward.
- Viewer examples: `!buy DemoBull 2`, `!sell DemoBull 1`, `!sell DemoBull all`, `!portfolio`, `!pf`, `!greetings good evening`, `!market`, `!top`.
- Broadcaster example: `!grant @amit1541 500`. The recorded Grant uses the dashboard confirmation form.
- Moderation examples: `!market pause`, `!market resume`, `!freeze @user`, `!unfreeze @user`, `!delist TICKER`.
- Reward defaults: 10 Coins per qualifying chat minute, 200 per Follow, 500 for an eligible first-time chatter within the five-minute Raid Visa window. Commands do not earn a Stipend minute.
