# Glossary map

- [Twitch Kit](packages/twitch-kit/GLOSSARY.md): the connection between the broadcaster's channel and the local app, including accounts, events, emotes, overlays and simulation.
- [Emote Exchange](apps/emote-exchange/GLOSSARY.md): the play-money market where a channel's emotes become Listings and viewers become Traders.

Twitch Kit supplies normalized Chat Events, Follows, Raids, stream status and the Emote Catalog. Emote Exchange interprets those inputs as market activity. Product rules belong to the Exchange engine, while networking, storage and delivery belong to the runtime.
